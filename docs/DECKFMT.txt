MERIDIAN.DAT  -  cropmod input deck
R. Halvorsen, 1994.  Amended 1996-05 (units note).

Column-exact.  Fields are read with the FORMAT statements in
cropmod.f; there is no tolerance for shifted columns.

RECORD 1  (FORMAT A8, I4, F8.3)
  cols  1- 8   station identifier, left justified, blank padded
  cols  9-12   calendar year
  cols 13-20   latitude, decimal degrees north

RECORD 2  (FORMAT F8.2, F8.2)
  cols  1- 8   available water capacity, mm water per mm soil
  cols  9-16   rooting depth, MILLIMETRES

  NOTE: record 2 field 1 is a dimensionless fraction and field 2 is in
  mm.  Their product is the profile capacity in mm.  Some of the older
  station files carry rooting depth in centimetres.  Those files are
  wrong and produce a profile ten times too small.

DAILY RECORDS  (FORMAT I3, F6.1, F6.1, F6.1, F6.2)
  cols  1- 3   day of year
  cols  4- 9   maximum air temperature, degC
  cols 10-15   minimum air temperature, degC
  cols 16-21   precipitation, mm
  cols 22-27   incoming shortwave radiation, MJ m-2 d-1

TERMINATOR
  A record with a negative value in cols 1-3.  Conventionally " -1".

Records must be in ascending day-of-year order.  This is not checked
by the model.  See MRD-118.
