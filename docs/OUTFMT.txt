MERIDIAN.OUT  -  cropmod output
R. Halvorsen, 1994.

LINE 1   banner
  MERIDIAN CROPMOD <version>  STN=<id>  YEAR=<yyyy>  NDAYS=<n>

LINE 2   column header, fixed text

LINES 3..N+2   daily records (FORMAT I4,1X,F7.2,1X,F7.3,1X,F7.3,1X,F7.1,1X,F7.3)
  cols  1- 4   day of year
  cols  6-12   soil water, mm
  cols 14-20   actual evapotranspiration, mm
  cols 22-28   drainage, mm
  cols 30-36   above ground biomass, g m-2
  cols 38-44   leaf area index

LAST LINE
  YIELD <F10.3>   grain yield, t ha-1

Fortran writes a field of asterisks when a value will not fit its
width.  Consumers must handle that.  A field that overflows can also
run into its neighbour with no separating space, which is why the
daily records must be read by column and not by splitting on
whitespace.  See MRD-143.
